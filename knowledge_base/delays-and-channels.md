# Delays and channels

## Channels
- App chat and email: answered within 1 business day.
- Phone: Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00.
- Branch: by appointment.

## Internal processing times
These are Sambre Bank rules, not legal deadlines.
- Replacement card: 3 to 5 business days.
- New PIN letter: 2 business days.
- Account closing: 10 business days after the request.
- Complaint acknowledgement: 2 business days.

## Customers abroad
The same channels work abroad. For a lost card the customer follows the steps on cardstop.be.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every question about a delay that already passed.
