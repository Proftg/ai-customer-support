# Online banking

## Setting up the app
1. Download the Sambre app and enter the customer number from the welcome letter.
2. Register the card reader or the phone as the signing device.
3. Choose a 5 digit app code. Never write it down, never share it.
The setup takes about 10 minutes.

## Signing a payment
Every payment above 30 EUR is signed in the app with the app code or the fingerprint. A payment the customer did not sign is not valid.

## Login problems
- Forgot app code: reset it in the app with the card and PIN, or ask a colleague for a new activation letter (3 business days).
- New phone: the old device is unlinked in the app settings, then the new one is registered as in step 2.
- Locked after 5 wrong codes: it unlocks after 30 minutes, or a colleague unlocks it after an identity check by phone.

## Safe use
The bank never asks for a PIN, an app code or a full card number by email, SMS or phone. A message that asks for one is phishing.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every reply about a blocked account.
