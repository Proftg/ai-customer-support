# Cards and accounts

## Products
Sambre Bank is a fictional Belgian retail bank. It offers three products:
- Sambre Current: a current account with a Maestro-style debit card, free to open, 2 EUR per month.
- Sambre Plus: the same account with a credit card, 5 EUR per month.
- Sambre Young: a free account for customers under 18, debit card with a 100 EUR weekly limit.

## What is in the card pack
A new card arrives by post in 5 to 7 business days. The PIN arrives in a separate letter two days later. The card must be activated with a first payment using the PIN at a terminal.

## Limits
- Debit card: 2,000 EUR per week for payments, 500 EUR per week for cash withdrawals.
- Credit card: 3,000 EUR per month.
- A customer can lower or raise a limit in the app, up to the maximum above.

## Lost, stolen or swallowed card
Tell the customer to call Card Stop on 078 170 170 right away, then block the card in the app. A replacement card is free and arrives in 3 to 5 business days.
Source: cardstop.be/fr-be/home, read on 2026-11-11.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every refund decision. Never promise a refund in a reply.
